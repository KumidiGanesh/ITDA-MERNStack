ABOUT BUS CONNECT

Connecting people with better bus information.

Bus Connect is a local bus information website concept designed for students, daily commuters, and rural passengers. Our goal is to make routes, stops, and timetable information easier to find.

Future versions can include official operator updates, verified schedules, and real-time bus tracking.
