About ITDA

The Integrated Tribal Development Agency (ITDA) works towards the overall development and welfare of tribal communities.

ITDA Paderu provides various development, welfare and public service activities for people living in tribal areas.

The PGRS portal provides a convenient digital platform where citizens can submit grievances and communicate their problems to the concerned authorities.

🎓
