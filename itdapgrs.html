About ITDA Paderu

Integrated Tribal Development Agency

Integrated Tribal Development Agency

ITDA Paderu is associated with development and welfare activities in tribal areas of the region.

The agency focuses on improving access to education, healthcare, infrastructure, livelihood opportunities, agriculture and other welfare services.

The Paderu and Araku region is well known for its rich tribal heritage, forests, coffee plantations, hills, valleys and waterfalls.

This website concept combines information about ITDA Paderu, Public Grievance Redressal Services and tourism information into one easy-to-use portal.

Discover Araku Valley

Araku Valley is one of the most beautiful hill regions of Andhra Pradesh, surrounded by green mountains, forests and valleys.

The region is famous for its coffee plantations, tribal culture, waterfalls, scenic viewpoints and pleasant hill-station environment.

Visitors can enjoy nature, photography, local food, tribal culture and the spectacular landscapes of the Eastern Ghats.
