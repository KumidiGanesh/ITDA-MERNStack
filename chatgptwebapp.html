Integrated Tribal Development Agency

About ITDA

The Integrated Tribal Development Agency works towards the development and welfare of tribal communities.

ITDA Paderu supports various development, welfare and public service activities in tribal areas.

The Public Grievance Redressal System provides citizens with a digital platform to submit their problems and grievances to the concerned authorities.

Major Services

🎓

Education

Supporting educational opportunities and development for tribal students.

🏥

Health

Supporting healthcare and welfare services in tribal areas.

🌱

Tribal Development

Promoting social and economic development of tribal communities.
